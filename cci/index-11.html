Inside The Investigation

How Cyber Crime Investigation Actually Works

Most people learn security tools. Investigators learn to think — to trace, to correlate, and to build a case that holds up.

Scene Identification

Every cyber crime starts with identifying what happened, what systems are involved, and what data may have been compromised or stolen.

Evidence Correlation

Connecting IP addresses, timestamps, email headers, log entries, and OSINT data into a coherent picture that points to a single actor or group.

Documentation Standards

What gets documented, how it's written, and why the format matters — especially when the report is handed to a police cyber cell or a judge.

CERT-In & Cyber Cells

How India's Computer Emergency Response Team operates, when to involve the police cyber cell, and what they need from a private investigator.

Legal Admissibility

What makes digital evidence legally admissible in court — and how poor evidence handling can destroy a case before it reaches trial.

Suspect Attribution

From anonymous IP to named suspect — the methodology investigators use to narrow down attribution, even through VPNs and proxies.
