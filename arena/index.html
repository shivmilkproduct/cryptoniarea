Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a normal CTF with logs and a flag?

No. Arena gives you a full case file — a complete, realistic evidence package — not a single log file and a flag to submit.

Do I need prior investigation experience?

No. The case is built to be worked through with a security fundamentals background, and you're evaluated on how you investigate, not on prior case experience.

Can I use AI to generate my report?

No. Arena is built to test your own analysis and reasoning. Reports are reviewed by our team around your own investigative work.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every submitted report is reviewed, and on successful completion you receive a verifiable Cryptonic Area Arena investigation certificate.

Will more cases be added?

Yes. The Arena is being built out with different cases across different fields — CA/0001 is the first of many.
