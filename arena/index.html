02What we provide

The Arena is a hands-on cyber investigation platform. Instead of lectures, you are handed a case and you work it the way a professional investigator would. Depending on the plan you choose, you receive:

  • A complete digital case file for each case you enroll in, covering case documents, authentication records, email records, network records, device records, witness statements and internal documents.
  • A report template and submission kit, so you can file your findings in a professional format.
  • Evaluation of your submitted report by an investigator, with rank and XP awarded on the leaderboard.
  • A shareable Certificate of Completion from Cryptonic Area Arena once you complete a case to the required standard.
  • With the Mentorship plan: step-by-step senior investigator guidance in addition to everything in Case Access.

03Simulated cases, built to feel real

Every case in the Arena is a simulated investigation. We design each one to feel exactly like a real incident: realistic logs, headers, timelines, device and network artefacts, conflicting witness statements and internal documents, all built from real-world attacker tactics and professional evidence formats. There are no sample logs and no flags to guess. You are expected to establish what happened on your own, the way an analyst would on the job.

That realism is deliberate. To keep the experience genuine and protect everyone involved:

  • All organisations, people, accounts, email addresses, IP addresses, devices and data inside a case are fictitious. Any resemblance to a real person, company or incident is coincidental.
  • Nothing in a case file is a real victim’s data, and no real system was compromised to create it.
  • Do not contact, probe, scan or attack any person, domain, IP address or system that appears in a case. Treat all of it as part of the exercise.
  • Do not present a case as a real incident, or report it to any authority, employer or third party as if it were real.
  • Case details, narratives and evidence may be updated by us at any time to keep cases fair and current.
