FAQ

How does a referral count?

A referral counts once the invited person creates an Arena account using your referral code or invite link.

What do I get for 15 referrals?

After your 15th successful referral, one full case with mentorship access is added to your account automatically, at no cost.

How do I share my code?

Copy your code or invite link, tap Share invite, or use the social icons at the top of this page. Your code is filled in for you.

Can I refer myself or use extra accounts?

No. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts and fake accounts are not counted towards your reward.

Where can I see who joined?

Your progress and the people who joined through your link are shown on this page, under Your progress and Recent activity.
