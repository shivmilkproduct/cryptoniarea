Refund & Cancellation Policy

All enrollments are final. Once a seat is confirmed, it is non-refundable.

Our programs run with strictly limited seats to preserve the quality of live, mentor-led instruction. The moment your seat is confirmed, that seat is reserved exclusively for you and is removed from availability — it cannot be offered to another student. Because of this, we are unable to reopen, resell, or refund a seat once it has been confirmed, regardless of whether you attend the sessions.

Why This Policy Exists

Limited seats exist to ensure every student gets real mentor attention. A confirmed seat that goes unused cannot be given to another student who may have been waiting for that exact spot — this is why cancellations after confirmation cannot be refunded.

This policy applies equally to all programs, Arena case unlocks, and any other paid service offered by Cryptonic Area, unless a specific offer explicitly states otherwise in writing.
