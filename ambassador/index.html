The Program

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can become a Student Ambassador?

Any currently enrolled student who is passionate about technology and cyber security and ready to lead a community at their campus. Prior security experience is not mandatory.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Ambassadors who complete the program requirements receive an official Cryptonic Area Student Ambassador certificate.

How do I apply?

Tap the Become Student Ambassador button. WhatsApp opens with a ready message to our team, who will share the complete process and the next steps.

How much time will I need to give?

Ambassadors dedicate a few focused hours every week to community activity and events. The exact expectations are shared during the process so you can plan around your studies.

Do I need to be from a technical background?

No. Students from any stream can apply. What matters is your leadership drive, communication and willingness to learn.

What support will I get from Cryptonic Area?

You are backed by the core team with onboarding, resources and guidance, and you join a network of fellow ambassadors to learn from.
