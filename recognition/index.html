What Is Recognition

A permanent, official record of achievement

Whenever a Cryptonic Area program concludes — an internship, hackathon, CTF, or workshop — the students and interns who performed well are officially recognized. This page exists as the single, trusted source for that record.

Recognition here isn't a participation badge. It's reviewed internally by the Cryptonic Area team once a batch ends, published to this portal, and kept as a lasting reference that recognized students can point to and that anyone can independently check.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Recognized" mean on this portal?

It means a student or intern successfully completed a Cryptonic Area program and met the performance standard required for official recognition — not simply that they registered or attended.

How are students selected for recognition?

The Cryptonic Area team reviews completion and performance once a batch concludes, and finalizes the recognized list internally before publishing it here.

Can employers or institutions verify a listing?

Yes. Each recognized batch links to its own dedicated list, and entries can be referenced directly against this official portal.

How often is this page updated?

A new batch is added as soon as a program concludes and its recognized list is finalized by the team.

Is there a fee to be recognized?

No. Recognition is earned through completion and performance in a Cryptonic Area program — it is never sold or purchased separately.

I was part of a batch but don't see my name — what do I do?

Reach out to the team via WhatsApp or email with your batch name and details, and it will be looked into directly.
